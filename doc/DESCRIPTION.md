Better Guesser is a self-hosted geography guessing game. You're dropped somewhere on real street-level imagery (from Mapillary), look around, and pin your guess on a world map.

Modes: Ranked duels (Bronze to Master with dynamic RP, then the Legend Division with MMR and a public leaderboard), plus 1v1 and 2v2 with matchmaking or private lobbies, and solo practice. Also includes player profiles, leaderboards, daily rewards and player reports.

The admin panel manages every account (rank, RP, MMR, roles, bans, passwords), handles reports, and has an admin-only cheat menu.
